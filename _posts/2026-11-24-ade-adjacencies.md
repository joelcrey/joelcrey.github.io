The contact simple singularities and their adjacencies, by characteristic [GK]. Switching to right equivalence shows which ones are right simple singularities [GN]; in characteristic $0$ the two classifications agree [Ar], [GN, §3]. An arrow $X \to Y$ means an unfolding $F_t$ with $F_0$ of type $X$ and $F_t$ of type $Y$ for $t \neq 0$; only arrows that are not compositions of others are drawn. Click a singularity for its normal form and an arrow for an unfolding. For $p \neq 2$, add a nondegenerate quadratic form in further variables [GK, 1.2], [GN, Thm. 3.2].

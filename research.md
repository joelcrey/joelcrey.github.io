My research has mainly focused on Singularity Theory in Positive Characteristic, which lies in the field of Commutative Algebra.

During my PhD, I have mostly been thinking about singularities and numerical invariants derived from the Frobenius endomorphism, also known as F-singularities and F-invariants, with a focus on various aspects of the Watanabe–Yoshida conjecture, one of the main driving questions in Hilbert–Kunz theory.

In this page, you can find a summary of some projects I have been working on, together with a brief explanation of some of the main results. Feel free to reach out if you have any comments!
